## Context

The problem and the forces at play: requirements, constraints, and the facts
that make a decision necessary now. Describe the situation neutrally, without
arguing for a solution yet.

## Decision

The decision, in active voice ("We use ...", "We will ..."), with enough detail
for someone to apply it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result, including costs,
risks, and any follow-up work the decision creates.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious option that was not chosen, and why.
